Frequently Asked Questions

What are tokens?

Tokens are units of AI computation. Roughly 1 token = 0.75 words. A typical conversation uses 100-500 tokens per message exchange.

Can I change plans anytime?

Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect immediately, and we'll prorate any billing differences.

What happens if I exceed my token limit?

Free users will be prompted to upgrade. Pro and Business users can continue with overage billing at standard rates.

Is there a free trial?

Yes! Pro and Business plans include a 14-day free trial. No credit card required to start.

Do you offer annual discounts?

Yes! Save 17% when you pay annually. Switch to yearly billing in your account settings.

What support do I get?

Free users get community support via Discord. Pro users get priority email support. Business users get 24/7 priority support with dedicated assistance.
